# Sharing your calendar link

Forward this to an owner who has not sent their calendar link yet. It is written to be
copied straight into an email, so it says nothing about EssentiaLyfe's software and
nothing an owner would need explained twice.

The links themselves are private and carry a secret code, so nothing can find one
automatically. The owner has to generate it, and it takes about thirty seconds.

---

## The email

You do not need to copy this by hand. Open any property, go to Availability, and press
"Email the owner". If that property has an email in its contact fields your mail client
opens already addressed and written. If it does not, the same text goes to your
clipboard so you can send it however you reach them.

It is deliberately short. An owner skims, and mail clients cut a pre-filled message off
at around two thousand characters.

Subject: Your calendar link

Hi,

Could you send me your calendar link, so your open dates update on their own? It takes
about thirty seconds and you only do it once.

The link shows which dates are taken and nothing else. It gives no access to your
account, and I cannot change anything or make a booking with it.

Airbnb: Calendar, pick the listing, then Availability, Connect calendars, Export
calendar.

Vrbo: Calendar, pick the property, then Sync calendars or Export calendar.

Google Calendar: Settings, click the calendar for this property, then Integrate
calendar, and copy the Secret address in iCal format.

Anything else: look for Export calendar, iCal link or Sync calendars in the calendar
settings.

It is a long address ending in .ics. If it starts with webcal instead of https, that is
fine.

If you cannot find it, send me a screenshot of that page and I will point at it.

Thank you,
Avi

---

## Notes for you, not for the owner

**The link is a secret address, not a login.** It is a long URL with a random code in
it. Anyone holding it can read the dates, so treat it like a password: do not put it
in a shared document.

**A link can go dead.** If an owner regenerates it, or moves the listing, the old one
stops working. The app tells you which property it happened on rather than quietly
blocking nothing, so you can ask for a new one.

**One property can have several.** If a home is on Airbnb and Vrbo, ask for both. The
app merges them, so a stay booked on one site does not show as free on the other.

**On Hold and Maintenance are not in any of these feeds.** No booking site publishes
them. You set those yourself on the property, and a calendar refresh will not wipe
them.

**Site menus move.** The steps above are written to describe what to look for rather
than an exact path, because Airbnb and Vrbo rearrange their settings regularly. If an
owner says they cannot find it, the screenshot offer is the fastest way through.
